==================================================================
QUICK VIVA POINTS
==================================================================
BFS: FIFO queue, shortest in steps, complete.  DFS: stack, less memory, not optimal.
UCS: expands lowest g(n); optimal for positive costs.
A*: f(n)=g(n)+h(n); optimal if h is admissible (never overestimates).
Greedy best-first: f(n)=h(n); fast but not optimal.
Hill climbing: may stop at local maxima, plateaus, ridges -> random restart.
Simulated annealing: accepts worse moves with prob e^(-delta/T), T decreases.
GA: population -> selection -> crossover -> mutation -> new generation.
CSP: variables, domains, constraints; backtracking + constraint checking.
Minimax: MAX picks max, MIN picks min. Alpha-beta prunes when alpha >= beta,
         same result, far fewer nodes.
Forward chaining: data-driven (facts -> conclusions).
Backward chaining: goal-driven (Prolog's default).
Resolution: add NOT goal, derive empty clause -> goal proved.
Naive Bayes: P(C|X) is proportional to P(C) * product of P(xi|C) (features independent).
Q-learning: Q(s,a) += alpha [r + gamma max Q(s',a') - Q(s,a)].
